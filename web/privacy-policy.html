Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Urayaha Days ("we", "us", or "our") operates GitTrend JP (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: We may collect information on how the Service is accessed and used, including your device type, browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and other diagnostic data.
  • Analytics Data: We use Google Analytics to understand how users interact with our Service. This includes anonymized information about your device, location (country/region level), and usage patterns.

2.2 User Data

  • Bookmarks: Your saved repository bookmarks are stored both locally on your device and synchronized to our cloud servers (Firebase) to enable cross-device access. A unique anonymous identifier (UUID) is generated to associate your bookmarks with your device.
  • Settings: Your app preferences (notification settings, language filter preferences) are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.
  • Anonymous Identifier: We generate a unique anonymous identifier (UUID) for each installation to manage bookmark synchronization. This identifier cannot be used to personally identify you.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve the Service
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues

4. Third-Party Services

Our Service uses the following third-party services:

  • GitHub: We display publicly available trending repository data from GitHub.
  • Google Analytics: For usage analytics. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Google Cloud): For backend services. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Vercel: For hosting. Vercel Privacy Policy

5. Data Retention

Locally stored data (bookmarks and settings) remains on your device until you clear your browser data or uninstall the app. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' standard retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Clear your locally stored data at any time through your browser settings
  • Opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using browser extensions or settings
  • Request information about data we may have collected

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
